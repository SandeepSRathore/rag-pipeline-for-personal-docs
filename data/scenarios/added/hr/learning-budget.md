# Learning and Development Budget

## Annual budget

Every employee has a learning budget of $1,500 per calendar year for courses, books, certifications and conferences. Unused budget does not carry over.

## Conference travel

Travel to one conference per year can be paid from the travel budget instead of the learning budget, with manager approval.

## Learning time

Employees may spend up to 4 hours per week on learning during working hours.

## How to claim

Submit receipts in Expensify under the "Learning" category. Purchases over $500 need manager approval first.
