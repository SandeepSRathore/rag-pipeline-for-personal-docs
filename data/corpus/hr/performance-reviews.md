# Performance Review Process

## Cycle

Lumora Labs runs performance reviews twice a year: in March (mid-year check-in) and September (annual review). Only the September review affects compensation.

## Steps

1. Self-review: each employee writes a 1-page self-assessment in Lattice.
2. Peer feedback: each employee picks 3 peers to give feedback; the manager may add up to 2 more.
3. Manager review: the manager writes the review and proposes a rating.
4. Calibration: managers in each department calibrate ratings together to ensure fairness.
5. Conversation: the manager shares the final review in a 1:1 within 2 weeks of calibration.

## Rating scale

Ratings are: Below Expectations, Meets Expectations, Exceeds Expectations and Exceptional.

## Promotions

Promotions are decided during the September cycle. A promotion case needs evidence of at least 6 months of consistent performance at the next level.
