# Remote Work Policy

Lumora Labs is a remote-first company with hubs in Lisbon, Toronto and Bangalore.

## Eligibility

All roles are eligible for fully remote work except Facilities and IT Hardware Support, which require 3 days per week on site at a hub.

## Working hours

Teams agree on at least 4 hours of overlapping core hours. For cross-region teams the default core hours are 14:00–18:00 UTC. Outside core hours, people work when it suits them.

## Working from another country

You may work from a country other than your country of employment for up to 30 days per calendar year. Longer stays need written approval from People Ops and Legal at least 4 weeks in advance, because of tax and immigration rules.

## Equipment and security

Use only your company-issued laptop for work. Do not work from public Wi-Fi without the company VPN (Tailscale). Lock your screen whenever you step away.

## Hub visits

Every employee may visit a hub or meet teammates in person up to 2 times per year; travel is booked under the Travel Policy.
