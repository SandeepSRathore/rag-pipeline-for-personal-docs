# Parental Leave Policy

All employees who become parents through birth, adoption or foster placement are eligible after 3 months of employment.

## Leave duration

Birthing parents receive 20 weeks of fully paid leave. Non-birthing parents receive 16 weeks of fully paid leave. Leave can be taken in up to 3 blocks within 12 months of the child's arrival.

## Phased return

After parental leave, employees may work 60% of their normal hours at full pay for 4 weeks.

## How to apply

Tell your manager and People Ops at least 8 weeks before the expected start date, then submit the leave request in Workday under "Parental Leave".
