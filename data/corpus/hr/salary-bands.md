# Engineering Salary Bands (Confidential)

HR and compensation committee only. Do not share outside the People team.

## Software Engineer bands (base salary, USD)

- L1 Associate Engineer: $95,000 – $118,000
- L2 Software Engineer: $118,000 – $145,000
- L3 Senior Software Engineer: $145,000 – $182,000
- L4 Staff Engineer: $182,000 – $225,000
- L5 Principal Engineer: $225,000 – $270,000

## Geographic adjustment

Bands are for the US. Toronto uses 0.85x, Lisbon 0.62x, Bangalore 0.45x.

## Equity

L3 and above receive an annual equity refresh worth 15% of base salary at target performance.
