# Paid Time Off Policy (2024)

This policy describes how full-time employees of Lumora Labs accrue and use paid time off (PTO).

## Annual allowance

Full-time employees receive 20 days of PTO per calendar year. PTO accrues monthly at 1.67 days per month, starting on the employee's first day. Part-time employees accrue PTO pro rata based on their contracted hours.

## Carryover

Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31, after which they expire. Unused days beyond the carryover limit are forfeited on December 31.

## Requesting time off

Submit PTO requests in the HR portal (Workday) at least 2 weeks in advance for absences of 3 days or more. Shorter absences need 2 business days' notice. Your manager approves or declines within 3 business days.

## Sick leave

Sick days are taken from the same PTO balance. If you are sick for more than 3 consecutive days, provide a doctor's note to HR.

## Public holidays

Lumora Labs observes 10 public holidays per year. The list is published on the HR portal each December.
