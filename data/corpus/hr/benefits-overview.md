# Employee Benefits Overview

## Health insurance

Lumora Labs covers 100% of health, dental and vision insurance premiums for employees and 75% for dependents. Coverage starts on the first day of employment. Open enrollment runs every November 1 to November 15.

## Retirement

We match 401(k) contributions dollar for dollar up to 5% of base salary. The match vests immediately.

## Wellness stipend

Every employee gets a wellness stipend of $100 per month for gym memberships, fitness classes, meditation apps or therapy co-pays. Submit receipts through the Expensify "Wellness" category.

## Home office stipend

New employees receive a one-time $750 home office stipend for a desk, chair, monitor or other equipment. After that, $300 per year is available for upgrades.

## Life and disability insurance

Life insurance at 2x annual base salary and long-term disability insurance are provided at no cost.

## Employee Assistance Program

The confidential EAP offers 8 free counseling sessions per issue per year for employees and household members. Call the number on the benefits portal, available 24/7.

## Contact

Benefits questions go to benefits@lumora-labs.example.
