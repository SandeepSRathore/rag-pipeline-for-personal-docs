# Business Travel Policy

## Booking

All business travel is booked through Navan at least 14 days in advance when possible. Bookings made less than 7 days before departure need VP approval.

## Flights

Economy class is the default. Premium economy is allowed for flights longer than 6 hours. Business class is allowed only for flights longer than 10 hours and needs VP approval.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel limit is $250 in most cities and $350 in high-cost cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Zurich, Tokyo).

## Meals and per diem

Meals while traveling are reimbursed up to a per diem of $75 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable except at approved client dinners.

## Ground transport

Use public transport or ride-sharing. Car rentals need manager approval and must be the compact category.

## Travel insurance

Business travel insurance is automatic for trips booked through Navan. In an emergency, call the 24/7 assistance line listed in the Navan app.
