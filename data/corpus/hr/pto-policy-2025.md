# Paid Time Off Policy (2025)

This policy replaces the 2024 Paid Time Off Policy from July 1, 2025. It applies to all full-time and part-time employees of Lumora Labs.

## Annual allowance

Full-time employees receive 25 days of PTO per calendar year, accrued monthly at 2.08 days per month. Employees with 5 or more years of service receive 28 days. Part-time employees accrue PTO pro rata.

## Carryover

Up to 10 unused PTO days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by June 30 of the following year. Days above the carryover limit are paid out at the employee's base daily rate in the January payroll.

## Requesting time off

Submit PTO requests in Workday at least 2 weeks in advance for absences of 5 days or more. Shorter absences need 2 business days' notice. Managers must respond within 2 business days; requests without a response are automatically approved.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is now separate from PTO. Every employee has 10 paid sick days per year that do not reduce the PTO balance and do not carry over. A doctor's note is required only after 5 consecutive sick days.

## Company-wide recharge week

The office closes for a recharge week between December 24 and January 1. These days do not count against PTO.
