# Lumora Insights Product FAQ

## What is Lumora Insights?

Lumora Insights is our analytics platform that turns product event data into dashboards, funnels and retention reports without SQL.

## Which data sources are supported?

Native connectors exist for Segment, Snowflake, BigQuery, PostgreSQL and Amplitude. Any other source can send events through the HTTP ingestion API.

## How long is data retained?

Raw events are retained for 25 months on the Growth plan and 7 years on Enterprise. Aggregated reports are retained indefinitely.

## Where is data hosted?

Customers choose EU (Frankfurt) or US (Virginia) hosting at sign-up. Data never leaves the chosen region.

## Is there a free trial?

Yes, a 14-day free trial of the Growth plan, no credit card required.

## Is Lumora Insights SOC 2 compliant?

Yes. Lumora Labs has a SOC 2 Type II report, renewed every year in October. Customers can request it through the Trust Center.
