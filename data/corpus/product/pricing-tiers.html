Lumora Insights Pricing Tiers

Prices are per month, billed annually. Monthly billing costs 20% more.

Starter

$49 per month. Up to 1 million events per month, 3 seats, 12 months of data retention, community support.

Growth

$399 per month. Up to 20 million events per month, unlimited seats, 25 months of data retention, SSO, and email support with a 1 business day response time.

Enterprise

Custom pricing, starting around $2,500 per month. Unlimited events, 7 years of data retention, SAML SSO and SCIM, a dedicated customer success manager, a 99.95% uptime SLA and 1-hour support response for P1 issues.

Discounts

Non-profits and educational institutions get 50% off Starter and Growth. Startups younger than 2 years with less than $5M in funding qualify for the Startup Program: Growth free for the first 12 months.
