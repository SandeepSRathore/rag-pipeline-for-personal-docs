# Lumora Labs Glossary

## Internal terms

- **Recharge week**: the company-wide office closure between December 24 and January 1.
- **Hub**: one of our three offices, in Lisbon, Toronto and Bangalore.
- **Lumi**: the internal name for the Lumora Insights web app.
- **Paved road**: the standard, supported way to build and deploy services, maintained by the Platform team.
- **IC**: Incident Commander, the person coordinating the response during a SEV1 or SEV2 incident.
- **DRI**: Directly Responsible Individual, the single owner of a project or decision.
- **Demo Day**: the monthly all-hands where teams show what they shipped, on the last Thursday of each month.

## Acronyms

- **ARR**: annual recurring revenue.
- **NRR**: net revenue retention.
- **SEV**: severity level of an incident (SEV1 is the highest).
