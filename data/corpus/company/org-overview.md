# Company and Organization Overview

## About Lumora Labs

Lumora Labs was founded in 2019 and builds Lumora Insights, a product analytics platform. We have about 240 employees across 18 countries.

## Leadership

- CEO: Priya Raman
- CTO: Daniel Okafor
- VP Engineering: Marta Silva
- VP People: Jordan Ellis
- CFO: Kenji Watanabe

## Departments

Engineering is organized into Product Engineering (Insights, Billing, Integrations teams), Platform (infrastructure, developer tooling) and SRE. Go-to-market includes Sales, Customer Success and Marketing. People Ops, Finance, Legal and Security report to the COO office.

## Company values

1. Customers first.
2. Write it down.
3. Small teams, big ownership.
4. Be kind and direct.
